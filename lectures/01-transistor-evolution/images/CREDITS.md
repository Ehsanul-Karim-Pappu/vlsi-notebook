# Image credits

The historical photos and documents shown in lecture 1. Each slide also carries a short credit
under the image.

Patent drawings come from the USPTO's full-text copies; US patent documents are free to
reproduce. They have been cropped to the relevant figure and toned to look like paper on the dark
slides. Photos have been resized, and some converted to greyscale. Adapted CC BY-SA images are
shared under the same licence as the original.
